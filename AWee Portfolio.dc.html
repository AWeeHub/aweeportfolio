Automations

The workflows behind the pages. Built in GoHighLevel so qualification, purchase branches, delivery, reminders and internal handoffs run without anyone chasing them.

SUITE 01
DFY Private Living Estate Trust — bundle
01GOHIGHLEVEL WORKFLOW

[Bundle] Application Submitted

Two survey triggers split disqualified from qualified applicants, then a condition routes each branch to its own tag and opportunity — so the pipeline reflects who actually qualified.

02GOHIGHLEVEL WORKFLOW

[Bundle] Bridge Sale Purchase

An order form submission on the DFY Trust funnel tags the buyer, creates the opportunity, sends email confirmation and SMS, then grants course access.

03GOHIGHLEVEL WORKFLOW

[Bundle] Purchase & Confirmation

Three triggers feed one condition with four outcomes — paid in full, payment option completed, deposit, and none — each running its own opportunity, welcome email, SMS and course grants.

05GOHIGHLEVEL WORKFLOW

[Bundle] Living Estate Trust Pre-Call — Reminders

A booked pre-call removes the AI tag and workflow, updates the opportunity, sends confirmation plus two internal notifications, then waits before the reminder email.

06GOHIGHLEVEL WORKFLOW

[Bundle] Payment Option Fully Paid

A contact tag marks the plan as settled, then sends the welcome, access and pre-call booking email with SMS and two course grants.

—GOHIGHLEVEL WORKFLOW

Consultation Bundle Session — Product Delivery

An order from the funnel updates the contact field, tags the buyer, sends email and SMS, then grants the two included products.

—GOHIGHLEVEL WORKFLOW

Track & Reduce Bundle Consults

Five calendar triggers meet a consult-credit check with branches for 3, 1 and 0 remaining — each decrements the field, texts the client and updates the opportunity, so bundle sessions can never be over-booked.

SUITE 02
EM — Master Your Rights event
01GOHIGHLEVEL WORKFLOW

EM | Optin Page

A form submission tags the lead and enrolls them into the nurture workflow.

02GOHIGHLEVEL WORKFLOW

EM | Master Your Rights — General Admission

The sales page order swaps the lead tag for a buyer tag and moves the contact into the attendee workflow.

03GOHIGHLEVEL WORKFLOW

EM | Master Your Rights — VIP Admission

Two order triggers separate one-click upsells from direct purchases, fire the Facebook Conversion API, then run opportunities, tags, email, SMS, course grant and a three-day reminder sequence per branch.
